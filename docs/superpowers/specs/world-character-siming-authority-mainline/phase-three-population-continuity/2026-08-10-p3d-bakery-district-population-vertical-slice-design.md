# P3D Bakery District Population Vertical Slice

Status: `design-only; implementation not authorized`

## Purpose And Scope

Prove P3 with a small bakery district: proprietor and staff, bounded customer
profiles, supplier contact, competitor and inspector. Individually embodied
roles resolve to authorized profiles; aggregate demand remains explicit policy
until P4. The slice uses P2 work/evidence, fixed or restricted public quotes,
existing production, inventory, payroll, Survival and permit/tax contracts.

## Acceptance Gate

Demonstrate activate/suspend/requeue, a contention rejection, due obligation,
scope-filtered public/private mirror, pause/catch-up and full/checkpoint-tail
replay. This must not claim price discovery, order books, cross-region trade,
macro economy or generic population simulation.
