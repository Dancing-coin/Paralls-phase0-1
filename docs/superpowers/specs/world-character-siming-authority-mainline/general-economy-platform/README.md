# General Economy Platform C Subsystems

The implementation is staged behind the approved v3/2.0 schema foundation.
Each subsystem keeps owner-local facts and requires an immutable descriptor,
precompiled recipe, focused tests, Harness evidence and full/checkpoint-tail
replay before activation.

1. Currency issuance and FX fixing
2. Account, ledger, hold and obligation
3. Quote, order and deterministic clearing
4. Commerce delivery and custody handoff
5. Organization labor and business period
6. Tax, permit and regulation
7. Credit, collateral, insurance and securities
8. Insolvency, clearing and audit
9. Regional macro close and Population market signals

No subsystem creates a generic writer, router, coordinator, registry,
settlement authority or second runtime.
