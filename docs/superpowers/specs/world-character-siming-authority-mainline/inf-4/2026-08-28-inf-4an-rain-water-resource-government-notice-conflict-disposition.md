## Disposition

Rejected. The source is valid Ecology truth, and the Government public-notice
stream exists, but no product requirement gives the recovered resource an
independent Government consequence. Creating this notice would establish a
pattern in which arbitrary Ecology state changes become Government notices by
merely naming a notice kind.

This would conflict with the finite public-workshop and public-milling notice
partitions. INF-3AA is a bounded owner-local water quantity correction; it does
not imply public service, permit, advisory, restriction, allocation,
attendance, payment, material custody, or notice.
