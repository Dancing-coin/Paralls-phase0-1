# P4D Commercial Ecosystem Vertical Slice

Status: `design-only; implementation not authorized`

## Purpose

Validate P4 with a small street: two bakeries, one supplier organization,
bounded customers, a landlord/service party and a regulator. It is a controlled
commercial ecosystem, not a city economy.

## Acceptance

Exercise public quote competition, procurement and delivery, work/labor
consequence, permit/inspection/tax due event, a bounded credit/default path and
one structured failure. Prove all final facts originate from existing owners,
every public/private view is scope-filtered, results explain the pinned policy
and full/checkpoint-tail replay agrees.

No macro claim is permitted; population and policy remain bounded to the P3/P4
reference package.
