# P4C Government, Credit And Public Obligations

Status: `design-only; implementation not authorized`

## Purpose

Combine existing permit, inspection, tax assessment, debt and contract
primitives into a small commercial-institution contract. Government owns policy
revision, permit and inspection outcomes; Economy/Debt own assessed amount,
claim, repayment and overdue result.

## Contract And Limits

A public policy view exposes version/digest, applicable jurisdiction, due
calendar, permit class and enforcement conditions. Inspection and credit are
typed proposals that pin subject and policy revision; authority validates
evidence, jurisdiction and grants before owner settlement. Credit is limited to
a bounded claim, repayment schedule, overdue state and declared consequence.

No monetary issuance, banks, securities, interest-rate market, exchange rate,
systemic risk, court or civilization public order is in P4.

## Gate

Prove policy revision invalidates stale proposals, inspection and tax trace to
evidence, credit default is explicit, private facts are filtered and replay is
identical.
