# P7A Civilization And Cross-Jurisdiction Projection

Status: `design-only; implementation not authorized`

## Purpose

Define a read-model vocabulary for scaled civilization capability and
cross-jurisdiction effects without building a civilization authority. Capability
changes, public order, infrastructure, production, finance and political/military
consequences remain owned by their future named domains and must originate in
committed facts.

## Contract

A versioned `CivilizationProjection` references source stream revisions,
jurisdiction scope, aggregation policy, lineage and uncertainty. It may compute
audience-filtered indicators and transmission explanations, but not write tax,
market, organization, character or territorial facts. Cross-jurisdiction input
is explicit, policy-pinned and classification-filtered.

## Gate

Prove source traceability, redaction, aggregation determinism, policy revision
and replay. Complete city, global market, war simulation, natural ecology and
automatic civilization generation are excluded.
