## Candidate Direction: Package-Defined Negotiated Exchange

The repository admitted one bounded outcome in which an active,
immutable gameplay-package revision declares a tradeable item or typed service,
its world eligibility (technology, institution, production/resource and other
domain-owned capability refs), allowed currency, and fixed or bounded price
policy. Character dossier data can produce an intent or a negotiated proposal;
party agreement is not settlement truth. Existing domain owners must still
provide the committed item/service/source evidence, and Economy must own the
fixed debit/credit or other admitted ledger vector.

This direction is documented in the Gameplay Domain Extension Catalog's
"Package-Provided Economic Content And Plausibility" section. It is not a
row approval and does not create a mod-owned economy authority, caller-open
policy registration, generic payment route, event family, or runtime write
path. An unavailable item (for example, a modern weapon in a world without the
declared technology/production conditions), an out-of-range negotiated price,
or an unpinned package/policy/source revision must remain zero-write.

The explicit approval named exactly one
`economic_outcome_id = package_declared_negotiated_exchange@1`, its source
owners/evidence kinds, the existing Economy operation, and terminal
no-compensation semantics. The approved Owner-Admission Contract pins event,
revision, privacy, idempotency, receipt, and replay details. This exact
implemented outcome does not admit another package outcome or weaken the
generic arbitrary-payment blocker.
